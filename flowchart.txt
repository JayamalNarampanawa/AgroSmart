Control Logic Clarification

AgroSmart uses a monitoring-first architecture with local control.

All irrigation decisions are executed directly on the ESP32 using predefined soil moisture thresholds to ensure reliable operation even during network outages.

AI-based precision agriculture components operate only at the dashboard level, where they analyze collected data to generate crop recommendations and advisory insights. These AI outputs do not directly control pumps or actuators.
